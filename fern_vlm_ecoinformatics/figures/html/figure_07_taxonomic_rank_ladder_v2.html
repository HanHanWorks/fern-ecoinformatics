Figure 7 v2. Fine-grained fern identification remains outside the reliable capability boundary

A taxonomic-rank ladder emphasizing that family-level correctness under the species prompt does not translate into genus- or species-level success.

Detailed content

This figure turns the species_l4 result into a rank-collapse view. Although the species prompt frequently preserves the correct family, the same outputs rarely identify the correct genus and almost never identify the correct species.

The figure therefore supports a boundary claim stronger than exact-match failure alone: even after collapsing predictions to genus, general-purpose VLMs remain unreliable for fine-grained fern classification.
