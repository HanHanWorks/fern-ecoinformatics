Figure 4 v2. Robustness checks under text masking and candidate-order perturbation

A quieter three-panel layout emphasizing paired comparisons, shuffled-order ranges, and prediction-change burden.

Detailed content

Panel A compares original and text-masked direct30 family accuracy on the same 60 images. Panel B shows that candidate-order shuffling changes performance even after visible text has been removed. Panel C quantifies the operational burden of that instability using changed-prediction counts and mean prediction entropy.

The figure supports a cautious robustness claim: the masking subset does not show a clear loss, but prompt-order sensitivity remains detectable in both accuracy ranges and sample-level prediction changes.
