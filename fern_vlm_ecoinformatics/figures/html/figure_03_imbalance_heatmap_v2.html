Figure 3 v2. Micro accuracy obscures family-level weakness under strong class imbalance

Journal-style three-panel summary of pooled accuracy, family-size structure, and rare-to-common family performance.

Detailed content

Panel A shows that pooled direct30 family accuracy (51.0%-60.6%) substantially exceeds macro family accuracy (24.8%-35.8%). Panel B explains the denominator structure: two dominant families contribute more than half of the local dataset. Panel C summarizes rare, medium, and common family macro recall for representative models.

The main interpretation is conservative: the best pooled score does not imply uniformly reliable family-level recognition across all 30 fern families.
